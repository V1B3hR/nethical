# Nethical Trademark & Brand Usage Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-09-15  
**Version:** 2.0  

---

## 1. Purpose

The "Nethical" name, the Nethical shield logo, and associated branding assets represent our commitment to mathematically verifiable, ethical, and safe artificial intelligence.

While the Nethical source code is licensed under the permissive [MIT License](LICENSE), the **Nethical trademark, trade names, and logos are NOT included in that copyright license**.

This policy clarifies what brand uses are permissible without prior written permission, and which require explicit authorization.

---

## 2. Permissible Uses (No Prior Approval Required)

You may use the Nethical wordmark without formal approval in the following factual, non-confusing contexts:

1. **Factual Statements of Compatibility:**
   - *"Compatible with Nethical v2.7.0"*
   - *"Integrated with the Nethical AI Governance Platform"*
   - *"Plugin for Nethical"*
2. **Academic & Research Publications:**
   - Citing Nethical in research papers, benchmarks, educational slide decks, or books.
3. **Linking to the Project:**
   - Linking directly to `https://github.com/V1B3hR/nethical` or official project domains.

---

## 3. Restricted Uses (Prior Written Approval Required)

You may **NOT** do any of the following without prior written consent from the Project maintainers:

1. **Forks & Derivative Products:**
   - You may not name a modified fork "Nethical", "Nethical Pro", "Nethical Enterprise", or similar names that imply official endorsement or upstream canonical status.
   - For example: *"Forked from Nethical"* is allowed, but *"Nethical Enterprise Edition"* by a third party is strictly prohibited.
2. **Domain Names & Social Media Handles:**
   - Registering domain names, social media handles, or company names containing "Nethical" (e.g., `nethical-security.com`, `@nethical_official`).
3. **Merchandise & Commercial Goods:**
   - Selling physical goods (apparel, hardware appliances) bearing the Nethical logo.
4. **False Claims of Certification:**
   - Stating or implying that your AI model, agent, or commercial service has been "Officially Certified by Nethical" unless an accredited audit and cryptographic verification has been performed.

---

## 4. Logo Usage Guidelines

- Do not alter the proportions, orientation, or core color schemes of the Nethical shield emblem.
- Do not combine the Nethical emblem with other corporate trademarks into a unified mark.
- Maintain adequate clear space around the logo in all presentations.
