# Nethical — Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

**Effective Date:** 2026-09-15  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Compliance Reference:** EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) Art. 5, US Executive Order 14110, NATO Responsible AI Principles  

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines mandatory behavioral guardrails and prohibitions for all users, deployers, and organizations integrating or operating the **Nethical** AI governance platform, SDK, or hosted services.

The purpose of Nethical is to guarantee the ethical alignment, safety, and cryptographic auditability of autonomous artificial intelligence. Using Nethical to mask, facilitate, or legitimize unlawful or hazardous AI activity is strictly prohibited.

---

## 2. Prohibited AI Practices (Strict Zero Tolerance)

In accordance with **Article 5 of the EU AI Act** and international human rights conventions, Nethical must **NEVER** be used to govern, assist, or operate:

1. **Subliminal & Manipulative Exploitation:**
   - AI systems deploying cognitive behavioral manipulation techniques beyond human consciousness to distort behavior and impair informed decision-making causing physical or psychological harm.
2. **Exploitation of Vulnerabilities:**
   - AI exploiting personal vulnerabilities related to age, physical or mental disability, or socio-economic conditions to manipulate individual autonomy.
3. **Social Scoring & Universal Profiling:**
   - Evaluating or classifying natural persons over time based on social behavior or personality traits leading to detrimental or unfavorable treatment in contexts unrelated to original data capture.
4. **Individual Criminal Risk Profiling:**
   - Assessing or predicting criminal offense risk based solely on profiling or personality traits without objective, verifiable factual evidence.
5. **Untargeted Facial Recognition Scraping:**
   - Creation or expansion of facial recognition databases through untargeted scraping of facial images from CCTV, biometric sensors, or the internet.
6. **Emotion Recognition in Prohibited Contexts:**
   - Inferring emotions of natural persons in workplace or educational environments, except where strictly required for verified occupational safety or medical diagnostics.
7. **Biometric Categorization of Sensitive Attributes:**
   - Inferring political opinions, trade union memberships, religious beliefs, race, or sexual orientation via biometric analysis.

---

## 3. Kinetic & Military Red Lines

In accordance with Fundamental Laws #2, #3, and #4:
1. **Unchecked Lethal Autonomy:** Nethical shall not be integrated into lethal autonomous weapons systems (LAWS) where kinetic targeting decisions occur without meaningful, verified human command authority.
2. **Critical Infrastructure Sabotage:** Deploying governed agents to execute unauthorized offensive cyber-attacks, physical sabotage against electrical grids, public water systems, or nuclear facilities.
3. **Biological, Chemical, or Radiological Threat Generation:** Utilizing models to design, synthesize, or weaponize CBRN agents.

---

## 4. Platform Security & Anti-Evasion Rules

Users and deployers shall NOT:
1. **Subvert the Merkle-DAG:** Attempt to forge, alter, or backdate cryptographic receipts within the Nethical immutable audit ledger.
2. **Adversarial Poisoning:** Intentionally train or fine-tune Nethical safety classifiers with poisoned datasets intended to blind governance detectors to malicious intent.
3. **Impersonation:** Circumvent digital signature checks by spoofing cryptographic identities or agent IDs.

---

## 5. Violations & Enforcement

1. **Immediate Revocation:** Any verified violation of this Policy results in immediate, permanent revocation of hosted API keys, enterprise licenses, and commercial support.
2. **Regulatory Reporting:** Where an intentional violation involves life-critical threats, terrorist activity, or illegal prohibited practices under the EU AI Act, Nethical maintainers reserve the right to notify competent market surveillance authorities and law enforcement bodies.
